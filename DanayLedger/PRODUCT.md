# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Accountants and financial managers at DANAY EXPRESS SARL, a logistics/transport company in West Africa. They use the app daily on desktop (primary) and occasionally on tablet to record revenue, expenses, truck income, and bank payments. Their workflow is data-heavy: rapid form entry, table scanning, status validation, and report generation. They work under time pressure during end-of-day closings and need clear status indicators and fast keyboard navigation.

## Product Purpose

DanayLedger is a financial management web app that centralizes daily revenue, expenses, truck revenue, and bank payments for DANAY EXPRESS SARL. It replaces paper-based and spreadsheet tracking with a structured, permission-controlled system. Success means: every transaction is recorded accurately, admins can validate and review in seconds, and reports give a clear picture of the company's financial health.

## Brand Personality

Serious, Reliable, Efficient. The interface should feel like a trusted tool, not a marketing surface. Trust comes from consistency and clarity. Efficiency comes from minimizing clicks and maximizing information density where it matters. No decorative motion; every animation serves a state change.

## Anti-references

- Over-designed fintech dashboards with gradient hero sections and animated counters
- SaaS marketing landing pages with glassmorphism cards and decorative illustrations
- Dark-mode-only dashboards that sacrifice readability for aesthetic
- Lightweight personal-finance apps (too playful, too consumer-oriented)

## Design Principles

1. **Show the data, not the chrome**: Tables, numbers, and statuses are the content. The UI should get out of the way.
2. **Keyboard-first speed**: Power users enter data fast. Tab order, focus indicators, and form shortcuts must be consistent and visible.
3. **Status at a glance**: Every record's state (en attente, validée, annulée) should be instantly recognizable without reading text.
4. **Consistent affordances**: Same button shape, same form control, same table row pattern across every module. One vocabulary.
5. **Progressive disclosure**: Filters and secondary actions are available but not dominant. The primary task is front and center.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA contrast minimum (4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text)
- Essential keyboard navigation: all forms and tables must be fully operable by keyboard
- Clear focus indicators on all interactive elements
- French-language UI; all labels and messages in French
- Touch targets minimum 44x44px on mobile
